Pilot Program

Validate fit, usage, and athlete feedback in 2–3 weeks.

Pilot batches are designed to reduce risk before full-room purchases. Start small, collect structured feedback, then scale.

Pilot Structure

Week 1

Confirm sizing spread and assign pairs by athlete profile.

Week 2

Track daily usage in drills and live go sessions.

Week 3

Review coach + athlete notes and choose scale-up tier.

Pilot Eligibility Checklist

  • Identified point person (coach/director/admin) for sizing and feedback
  • Roster estimate available (target pilot pair count)
  • Preferred decision window (within current or next season)
