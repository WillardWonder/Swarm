FAQ

Questions coaches and parents ask before ordering.

Is MatWalker for competition or training?

MatWalker is currently positioned as training-first footwear for daily room sessions and drill work.

Can we run a pilot first?

Yes. Most programs begin with a 2–3 week pilot batch, then decide on a larger tier once fit and usage are validated.

How does team pricing work?

Pricing is based on pair-count tiers (10, 25, 50+). Submit your estimated pair count to get the current grid.

What info should we gather before requesting pricing?

Have your estimated roster count, age range, and desired rollout timeline ready. That speeds up recommendations.

How quickly can a team start after joining the waitlist?

Most teams can move from pricing request to pilot planning quickly once roster size, role mix, and timeline are confirmed.

Can parents and coaches submit together?

Yes. Coaches, parents, and program directors can all submit interest. Include the team/program name so requests can be coordinated.

Do you support school or booster-club purchasing workflows?

Yes. Teams can use MatWalker pricing details for school approvals, booster planning, and parent-funded order coordination.

How should we handle sizing for mixed age groups?

Start with your current roster by age/weight class and note expected growth-season changes. A pilot batch helps validate sizing mix before a larger order.

How durable is MatWalker for daily practice volume?

MatWalker is designed for repeated training use. Teams typically validate durability and cleaning workflow during the pilot period before scaling.

Do we need to pay to join early access?

No. Waitlist and pricing requests are non-binding and require no payment.
