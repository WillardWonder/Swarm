# Manufacturer Negotiation Playbook (MatWalker)

## Primary objective
Get reliable production at the lowest landed cost without sacrificing quality.

## Minimum data required before negotiation
- MOQ
- Sample fee + sample lead time
- Unit pricing at 100 / 500 / 1000 pairs
- Mold/tooling fees (if any)
- Production lead time after deposit
- Payment terms
- Incoterms (EXW/FOB/DDP)

## Negotiation sequence
1. **Anchor with volume bands**
   - Ask for tiered pricing in one shot (100/500/1000) to reveal curve.
2. **Bundle asks**
   - Negotiate unit price + sample fee waiver + faster lead time together.
3. **Competitive pressure (ethical)**
   - State you are comparing multiple qualified factories this week.
4. **Risk swap**
   - Offer higher repeat probability for better first-order terms.
5. **Close with conditional commitment**
   - "If you confirm X price + Y lead time + Z QC terms, we can move to sample immediately."

## Status values for CRM
- new
- contacted
- quoted
- negotiating
- sample-ordered
- sample-approved
- pilot-order
- inactive
- rejected

## WhatsApp correspondence checklist
- Keep message concise and numeric.
- Confirm every quoted number in writing.
- Ask for factory address + legal entity + business registration.
- Request product photos/video with date marker.
